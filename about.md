    <h1>About</h1>
  </header>

  <div class="page-content">
    <p>I'm Matthew Zbikowski — a software engineer who writes about the things that slow me down long enough to think: tools, language, the texture of how ideas move between minds.</p>

    <p>By day I build software. Outside of that I read, take notes, and occasionally write something I feel is worth publishing here.</p>

    <p>The name of this site, <em>zbikow.ski</em>, is a compressed version of my surname — a small joke about Polish diminutives and vanity domains.</p>

    <h2>What this is</h2>

    <p>A personal site. Essays, mostly. Long-form notes on software, writing, and cognition. No newsletter, no comments, no tracking. Posts appear when they're ready.</p>

    <h2>Contact</h2>

    <p>The best way to reach me is by email: <code>m [at] zbikow.ski</code>. I read everything but reply selectively.</p>
